Deleting your data — Go Kifu

Русская версия · Privacy Policy

Go Kifu has no user accounts and no developer servers, so there is nothing to delete “on our side” — it was never there. Everything created in the app is deleted by you, completely:

  • A single game — swipe it right-to-left in the list, then “Delete”. A folder or a tournament is deleted together with its games.
  • The federation rating list — Settings → Rating list → “Delete from device”.
  • The club pass — Settings → My club → “Forget the pass”.
  • Lists of players, tournaments and countries — swipe the row in the matching settings section.
  • Everything at once — uninstall the app, or Device settings → Apps → Go Kifu → Storage → Clear data.
  • The copy in your own cloud (if you enabled Yandex.Disk sync) — delete the app folder on your Disk; the “Sync” section also deletes a single game from the cloud.

There is no need — and no place — to send a deletion request to the developer: the developer never receives your data. Questions: weaver1982@yandex.ru.
