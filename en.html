Privacy Policy — Go Kifu Editor

Effective date: 17 August 2026

Русская версия

Summary

Go Kifu Editor (the “App”) records and reviews games of Go. The App has no servers of its own and no user accounts: your records stay on your device. The developer does not collect, receive or store your data. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking in the App.

Some features reach the internet when you choose to use them — your own cloud storage and publicly available sources. The full list is below.

What is stored on the device

Only what you create or request yourself:

  • game records in SGF format: player names, ranks and ratings, tournament names, dates, times, venues, results, comments;
  • the lists of players and tournaments you fill in;
  • app settings (theme, board colour, language, tournament parameters and so on);
  • when you use the matching features — the downloaded rating list, the cached catalogue of professional games, the club pass code.

All of it lives in the App’s private storage on the device. Backups and .sgf files are written only to a folder you choose yourself.

When the App uses the internet

Every request happens because of your action or a setting you turned on. In no case is anything sent to the developer — the App has no server.

  1. Sync with Yandex.Disk (only if you enable it). You sign in to your own Yandex account and the App stores a copy of your game library, lists and settings in the App folder on your Disk. The data stays with you, in your cloud; Yandex defines how it processes it. The access token is kept on the device only, is never synced to other devices, and is deleted when you sign out. Turning sync off and deleting the copy are both in the settings.
  2. Rating list of the Russian Go Federation (only when you press the button). The App downloads the public rating list as a whole and keeps it on the device so that player search works without a network. The request contains none of your data. The list is updated manually only and can be deleted with one button.
  3. Professional games (when you open that section). The App downloads a public catalogue and the game records from a publicly available source (gokifu.com). None of your data is sent.
  4. Club pass (only if you activated one). The App stores the pass code you received personally at the club and requests only public information from the club server — the schedule and the club name, without authentication. The App never requests member data: no name, no membership, no visit history is requested or stored. The pass code never leaves the device and is not included in sync.
  5. Update check (RuStore build only). The build distributed through RuStore asks the RuStore app installed on the device whether a newer version exists and offers to update. The AppGallery build has no such check at all — updates come from the store itself.

Camera

Camera access is used for two things: scanning the club pass code and recognising a position from a physical board. Frames are processed on the device, are never transmitted and are not stored, apart from the game you are recording. Code recognition works offline. The permission is requested at the moment you use these features; declining it does not affect the rest of the App.

What the App does not do

  • does not collect analytics, usage statistics or device identifiers;
  • does not show advertising and does not use advertising identifiers;
  • does not use tracking SDKs and does not pass data to third parties for marketing;
  • does not require registration and creates no accounts on the developer’s side;
  • does not handle payments.

Data you share yourself

If you send a game file with “Share”, or save it to a folder or a cloud of your choice, what happens to that file next is governed by the rules of the service or app you picked. Go Kifu Editor only hands the file to the recipient you chose.

Children

The App is not directed at children specifically and collects no data about users of any age.

Changes to this policy
